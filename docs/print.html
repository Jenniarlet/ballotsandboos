Rulebook House & Land City Machine
  1. 1Food Security Fund. 0.5% of property tax revenue goes to grants for local food producers, with no rate increase.YN?
  2. 2New preamble. New opening statement on ʻāina, kūpuna and accountable government. Legal powers stay the same.YN?
  3. 3Office of ʻŌiwi Resources. A cultural stewardship office in the Mayor's office, plus a Cultural Advisory Council.YN?
  4. 4LGBTQIA+ Commission. A 9-member commission advising the Mayor, Council and city agencies.YN?
  5. 5Ranked-choice voting. Rank up to 5 for Mayor, Council, Prosecutor. 2032 at the earliest, only if the Legislature approves.YN?
  6. 6HPD & immigration. Protect rights regardless of status. Help federal enforcement only when law or a court order requires.YN?
  7. 7Housing as city policy. Increasing and preserving housing becomes stated policy and a compelling government interest.YN?
  8. 8Affordable Housing Fund. Repayments and interest flow back in. Mixed-use and mixed-income projects qualify.YN?
  9. 9Property hearings. Drops the separate public hearing for city real estate deals. Still on Council agendas.YN?
  10. 1010-year housing plan. Required starting 2030, updated at least every 5 years.YN?
  11. 11Grants to nonprofits. Clean Water and Climate Resiliency funds can give grants to nonprofits.YN?
  12. 12Outside funding. City special funds can accept federal, state and philanthropic money.YN?
  13. 13Historic preservation. The Clean Water & Natural Lands Fund can also pay for historic preservation, and is renamed.YN?
  14. 14Land upkeep cap. Upkeep for fund-bought land rises from 5% to 10% of last year's deposits (about $440k to $880k).YN?
  15. 15Office of Data & Innovation. A city office for data, privacy and responsible AI use, written into the Charter.YN?
  16. 16Green card holders on boards. Permanent residents living in Honolulu can serve on most city boards.YN?
  17. 17Quorum rules. Vacant and non-voting seats don't count toward quorum. Neighborhood Boards unaffected.YN?
  18. 18HR First Deputy. Becomes an appointed position outside civil service, starting 2027.YN?
  19. 19Charter Commission support. The City Clerk staffs the Charter Commission, which meets every 10 years.YN?
  20. 20Ban after impeachment. An impeached Mayor, Councilmember or Prosecutor can't hold city office for 2 years.YN?
